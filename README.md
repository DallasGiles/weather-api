Weather Dashboard

Description

As a traveler, having accurate and up-to-date weather information for multiple cities is crucial for planning trips effectively. This Weather Dashboard application allows users to search for the current and future weather conditions of any city, helping them to make informed decisions about their travel plans. The application stores search history for easy access to previously searched cities, ensuring users can quickly view weather updates.

Features

	•	City Search: Enter the name of any city to view its current and future weather conditions.
	•	Current Weather:
	•	Displays the city name and the current date.
	•	Provides an icon representation of the current weather conditions.
	•	Shows the current temperature, humidity, and wind speed.
	•	5-Day Forecast:
	•	Displays the upcoming five days of weather for the searched city.
	•	Each day’s forecast includes the date, an icon representing the weather conditions, temperature, wind speed, and humidity.
	•	Search History:
	•	Keeps a record of searched cities.
	•	Allows users to click on a city from the search history to view updated weather conditions.

Acceptance Criteria

	•	City Search:
	•	GIVEN a weather dashboard with form inputs
	•	WHEN I search for a city
	•	THEN I am presented with current and future conditions for that city, and that city is added to the search history.
	•	Current Weather Conditions:
	•	GIVEN I view current weather conditions for a city
	•	THEN I am presented with the city name, the date, an icon representation of weather conditions, the temperature, the humidity, and the wind speed.
	•	Future Weather Conditions:
	•	GIVEN I view future weather conditions for a city
	•	THEN I am presented with a 5-day forecast that displays the date, an icon representation of weather conditions, the temperature, the wind speed, and the humidity.
	•	Search History Functionality:
	•	GIVEN I click on a city in the search history
	•	THEN I am again presented with current and future conditions for that city.



Usage

	1.	Search for a City:
	•	Enter the name of the city you want to check the weather for in the search input field.
	•	Press Enter or click the search button to view the weather details.
	2.	View Current Weather:
	•	The dashboard will display the current weather conditions, including temperature, humidity, and wind speed, along with an icon that represents the weather.
	3.	View 5-Day Forecast:
	•	Scroll down to see the 5-day weather forecast, including detailed weather information for each day.
	4.	Access Search History:
	•	Previous searches are saved in the search history.
	•	Click on any city in the history to view its current and future weather conditions.

Technologies Used

	•	Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
	•	API: OpenWeatherMap API
	•	Storage: Local Storage for saving search history
